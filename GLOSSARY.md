# Expense.io

Expense.io groups users by company and manages receipt-backed expense notes. The vocabulary below describes the product's people and records.

## Language

**Company**:
An organisation grouping users and their expense notes.

**User**:
A person with an Expense.io account and an assigned role.

**Employee**:
A user with the employee role.
_Avoid_: Submitter when referring specifically to the employee role.

**Submitter**:
The user who submits their own expense note. A submitter can also hold the manager or human-resources role.

**Manager**:
A user assigned to supervise other users' expense notes.

**Root manager**:
A manager who has no assigned manager.

**Human-resources administrator (RH)**:
A user responsible for the company directory and decisions on root managers' expense notes.
_Avoid_: Finance administrator, which is not an established role here.

**Expense note**:
A record of an expense's amount, date, description, receipt reference and decision state.
_Avoid_: Receipt when referring to the expense record.

**Receipt**:
The supporting document attached to an expense note.

**Pending**:
The state of an expense note awaiting a decision.

**Approved**:
The state of an expense note accepted by a reviewer.

**Rejected**:
The state of an expense note refused by a reviewer.
